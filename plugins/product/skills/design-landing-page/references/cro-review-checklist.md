# CRO Review Checklist

Use this checklist for landing page audits, redesign prioritization, and optimization planning. Treat every recommendation as a hypothesis unless the evidence is already strong.

## Conversion Goal

- Is there one primary action the page is designed to produce?
- Does the CTA match the user's buying stage and traffic source?
- Can success be measured with a clear metric such as signups, demo requests, purchases, downloads, or qualified leads?
- If testing is requested, express the hypothesis as: "We aim to [goal] by testing [change] on [page]. We expect [metric] to improve because [reason]."

## Buyer-Centered Diagnosis

- **Trust:** Does the page make the business, product, and claims feel credible?
- **FUDs:** What fears, uncertainties, or doubts could stop action?
- **Incentives:** Why should the visitor act now instead of later?
- **Engagement:** Does the page create enough relevance, clarity, and emotional connection to continue?
- **Persona temperament:** Does the page support fast scanners, proof-seekers, risk-averse buyers, and detail-oriented evaluators?
- **Buying stage:** Is the page written for awareness, consideration, comparison, purchase, or expansion?
- **Sale complexity:** Does the page provide enough education and proof for the size of the decision?

## Copy And Offer

- Is the headline specific, benefit-led, and tied to the visitor's problem or desired outcome?
- Is the offer easy to understand in under a few seconds?
- Does the copy speak to customer needs rather than internal product language?
- Are features connected to concrete outcomes?
- Are claims supported by proof, examples, or product visuals?
- Are there avoidable spelling, grammar, formatting, or tone issues that reduce trust?

## CTA And Navigation

- Is the primary CTA visible in the hero and repeated after high-intent sections?
- Is CTA copy action-oriented and outcome-aware?
- Are CTA color, size, placement, and surrounding whitespace strong enough to signal priority?
- Is navigation simplified for the page goal?
- Are secondary links clearly subordinate and useful to the same conversion path?

## Proof And Trust

- Are testimonials specific and credible?
- Are logos, ratings, case studies, certifications, awards, security details, privacy notes, or press mentions used truthfully?
- Is proof placed close to the claims it validates?
- Does the page explain what happens after conversion, especially for demos, trials, waitlists, and paid offers?
- Are pricing, cancellation, guarantees, support, privacy, or implementation expectations clear enough for the audience?

## Visual Design And UX

- Is the visual direction intentional and brand-consistent?
- Does the first viewport contain a clear promise, CTA, and reason to continue?
- Is the visual hierarchy obvious on mobile and desktop?
- Do product screenshots, videos, or images explain the offer rather than decorate it?
- Does the page avoid clutter, competing CTAs, and generic template patterns?
- Are mobile tap targets, line lengths, section spacing, and text sizes comfortable?
- Are contrast, alt text, keyboard access, and reduced-motion expectations respected?

## Measurement And Testing

- Use A/B tests for focused changes such as headline, CTA copy, CTA placement, offer framing, hero visual, proof placement, pricing display, and navigation simplification.
- Use heatmaps or session recordings to identify attention, rage clicks, dead clicks, scroll drop-off, and ignored CTAs.
- Use analytics to check page speed, traffic source quality, device differences, conversion funnel drop-off, and lead quality.
- Prioritize tests by likely conversion impact, evidence strength, effort, and risk.
- Do not test many unrelated changes at once unless intentionally running a full redesign experiment.

## Performance

- Check above-the-fold load speed, image weight, font loading, third-party scripts, tracking scripts, video embeds, and unused code.
- Compress and properly size media.
- Keep animation supportive and lightweight.
- Validate the page on real mobile widths, not only desktop previews.
